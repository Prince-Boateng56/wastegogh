About WasteGoGH

Revolutionizing Ghana's Waste & Sanitation Infrastructure

The Sanitation Challenges We Are Solving

Overcoming traditional waste collection friction in Ghanaian urban centers

Urban Waste Accumulation

Overflowing municipal bins and uncollected waste cause severe environmental degradation and health risks across Accra and Kumasi.

Irregular Pickups

Traditional collectors lack fixed schedules or live updates, leaving families waiting days without knowing when waste will be collected.

Lost Recycling Value

Valuable PET plastics, metals, and paper end up in landfills due to absence of incentivized sorting and direct recycling channels.

Communication Gaps

No direct, transparent communication between waste generators and licensed collectors to resolve missed pickups or issue feedback.
